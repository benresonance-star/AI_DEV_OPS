Section 0

About Plasma

Plasma is not a geometry engine with analysis added. It is a reality-refinement engine whose world can sometimes be viewed as geometry.

Its job is to maintain an evolving, computable model of a real or proposed world; understand what is known, what is derived, what is uncertain, what depends on what, and what is worth resolving next. Architecture is the first demanding application domain, but the underlying model is broader than buildings.

0.1

Why Plasma exists

Architectural design is currently split across modelling, BIM, planning, environmental analysis, structure, services, cost, rendering and specialist simulation. Each tool often has its own representation, timing and assumptions. Analysis commonly arrives after important design decisions have already been made.

Fragmented truthGeometry, BIM, analysis and survey data drift into parallel models.
Late feedbackSpecialist tools often check a design after the design has hardened.
Expensive recomputationSmall changes can trigger large rebuilds because dependencies are poorly expressed.
Geometry-first thinkingMeaning, constraints, uncertainty and provenance are treated as attachments to shapes.
AI without state disciplineLLMs are useful proposers but unreliable as owners of canonical technical state.
Too few explored worldsHuman teams cannot manually generate and assess enough credible alternatives early.
0.2

What Plasma solves

One evolving world, many representations.A building, site or landscape has stable identity while BReps, meshes, fields, splats, BIM projections and reports remain replaceable views.
Selective recomputation.Changes invalidate only what actually depends on them; lazy updates, tiling and progressive resolution keep interaction immediate.
Safe machine action.Humans, agents, generators and solvers propose typed operations; deterministic validation decides whether canonical state changes.
Earlier specialist intelligence.Wind, daylight, energy, structure, cost and compliance can become callable evaluations during design rather than end-stage checks.
Exploration without losing lineage.Alternative worlds can fork, evaluate and preserve diverse high-performing solutions instead of repeatedly overwriting one model.
Measured and designed reality in one framework.Survey, sensors, scans, point observations and inferred conditions can coexist with proposed geometry and explicit confidence.
0.3

What value it creates

Faster decisionsImmediate proxies first; exact and expensive truth follows only where required.
More credible optionsExplore far more design worlds while preserving compliance, performance and provenance.
Less reworkExpose constraints and specialist consequences while designs are still fluid.
Better AI leverageAgents operate through bounded contracts and verifiable state instead of free-form model mutation.
Tool independenceOpenCascade, Z3, simulators, USD and future engines can be replaced behind stable operation contracts.
Compounding capabilityEach verified operator becomes reusable infrastructure for later workflows and autonomous refinement.

Examples of use

Development feasibilityImport a site and planning controls; generate and compare compliant envelopes, yield, apartment layouts, parking, cost and solar outcomes.
Live architectural designChange massing, floor plates, cores or façade and progressively update geometry, areas, daylight, structure, energy and cost.
Landscape + ecologyModel terrain, hydrology, vegetation, soil, groundwater and environmental fields at different spatial resolutions.
Existing-world reconstructionCombine surveys, scans, splats, point observations and semantic inference without pretending every observation is exact truth.
Design ecologyFork thousands of candidate worlds, cheaply reject weak ones, deeply evaluate survivors, and preserve diverse high-performing families.
Specialist co-designProject the relevant part of the world to structural, wind, thermal, acoustic or fabrication tools and bring results back as typed claims.

The smallest useful mental model

WORLDEntities + Facts + RepresentationsWhat exists, what is known about it, and how it can currently be expressed.
CHANGEOperationsTyped procedures that read state and propose a candidate patch.
HISTORYRevisionsAccepted changes remain traceable and reproducible.
COMPUTATIONDependency DAGDerived values declare what they read and write so stale state can be localized.
TRUTH GATEValidation + constraintsCandidate results must prove validity before canonical state changes.
INTERACTIONProgressive world resolutionCheap proxy now; refined and exact representations catch up progressively.
Kernel bias: prefer the fewest stable primitives possible. Current working primitives are Entity, Fact, Representation, Operation. Relations can be typed facts; events can be committed operations; constraints can be evaluating operations.

0.7

How Plasma should be built

Build by verified capability frontier, not by completing a giant platform architecture. The north-star architecture constrains evolution; real workflows decide what gets implemented next.

1Pick a real questionWhat architectural task cannot Plasma do yet?
2Delete and simplifyMake the requirement earn every new abstraction and technology.
3Build one vertical slicePrefer a visible end-to-end capability over a horizontal subsystem.
4Verify deterministicallyCompiler, geometry validity, known test worlds, numerical checks, constraints and regression tests outrank LLM judgment.
5Commit only proven gainsPreserve capability in Git and the capability ledger; reject or revert failed experiments.
6Repeat at the frontierAdd fields, Z3, schedulers, MAP-Elites or distributed execution only when a measured bottleneck demands them.
Agentic RAD loop: Selector → Builder → deterministic tests → Verifier → accept / retry / stop. The development loop that improves Plasma should mirror the Plasma world loop: propose → execute → evaluate → commit.
0.8

North-star invariants for humans and AI

World owns state.No UI, agent, geometry kernel or external solver silently becomes a second canonical model.
LLMs propose; they do not declare truth.Machine-generated changes pass through typed operations and validation.
Authority is domain-specific.OCC may own exact geometry while the world graph owns semantic identity and a simulator owns a derived claim.
Representations are replaceable.Stable entity identity must survive changes of mesh, BRep, field, splat or external format.
Dependencies are explicit.Never use global recomputation when the real dependency chain can be expressed.
Approximation is labelled.Provisional, stale, approximate and verified state must never be confused in the UI or API.
Failures are local.A failed analysis should invalidate its dependents, not destroy unrelated world state.
Complexity must be earned.Do not build speculative infrastructure because it sounds appropriate for a world engine.
Before an AI changes Plasma, it should answer
  1. What concrete user capability will this change prove?
  2. What is the smallest implementation that can prove it?
  3. Can existing primitives or operators do the job without new infrastructure?
  4. What does the operation read, write and invalidate?
  5. What deterministic test proves success and protects prior capability?
  6. Does this alter a north-star invariant, canonical schema, security boundary or geometry authority? If yes, stop for human review.
